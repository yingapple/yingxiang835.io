description: "BrowseComp-ZH is our new benchmark for AI’s “second half,” inspired by Shunyu Yao’s essay that argues future wins will hinge on tougher, real-world evaluations rather than bigger models. The dataset packs 289 multi-hop Chinese-web questions across 11 domains, each with a single verifiable answer, and quality-controlled to ensure high difficulty. When we ran 20-plus leading LLM agents, most scored under 10 percent and the best only 42.9 percent—evidence that true “Chinese web surfing” remains hard. Everything—paper (arXiv 2504.19314), code, data, and auto-eval scripts—is Apache-2.0 on GitHub (PALIN2018/BrowseComp-ZH). Try it on your own model and join the leaderboard battle."
